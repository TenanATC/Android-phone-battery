# Battery Analyzer

An Android app that evaluates your phone's battery life and consumption, then
suggests concrete, device-specific ways to get more hours per charge.
Everything runs on-device — no network access, no accounts, no data leaves the
phone.

## How it's "tuned to your phone"

- **OEM detection at runtime.** The app identifies your manufacturer (Samsung,
  Google/Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus/Oppo, Motorola, Huawei, Sony, Nothing, Asus, …)
  and phrases every suggestion in terms of *your* settings layout — e.g. it
  points a Samsung user at *Battery protection* and *Deep sleeping apps*, and a
  Pixel user at *Adaptive Battery* and *Charging optimization*.
- **Material You.** On Android 12+ the app adopts your wallpaper's color
  palette.
- **Real readings, not generic advice.** Every suggestion is triggered by an
  actual measurement on your phone: your screen-timeout value, your brightness
  mode, your sampled drain rates, your charging temperatures, your app usage.

## Features

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Battery** | Live level, charge state, current draw (mA), temperature, voltage, health flag, charge cycles (Android 14+), estimated real capacity (mAh), and drain rates split screen-on vs screen-off |
| **History** | Battery level chart over 24h/48h/7d with charging segments highlighted, plus pattern stats (deep discharges, hours held at 100%, charging temperatures) |
| **Advice** | Prioritized suggestion cards generated by a rule engine, each with a one-tap deep link into the relevant Settings screen |
| **Apps** | Apps ranked by foreground time over the last 7 days (requires the "Usage access" special permission) |

## How it works

- A `WorkManager` periodic job samples the battery every 15 minutes (the
  platform minimum) into a local SQLite database; a runtime
  `ACTION_BATTERY_CHANGED` receiver adds finer-grained samples whenever the
  process is alive, and every app launch adds one too. Sampling reads a
  broadcast Android is already sending, so it wakes no radio, GPS, or sensor.
- Because OEM background management (notably One UI's app sleep) defers the
  periodic job for hours, the app reports its own sampling health and offers a
  one-tap route to the battery-optimisation exemption when readings are sparse.
- Charge state is derived from `EXTRA_PLUGGED`, not `EXTRA_STATUS`:
  `BATTERY_STATUS_FULL` latches after a completed charge and can persist once
  unplugged, and `BATTERY_STATUS_NOT_CHARGING` is reported while plugged in
  with charging paused (charge caps, thermal throttling). Reading charge state
  from the status flag mislabels discharges as charges.
- `BatteryAnalyzer` converts the sample history into drain rates (%/hr,
  screen-on vs screen-off), a time-remaining estimate, deep-discharge counts,
  time held at 100% while plugged, and temperature patterns.
- `SuggestionEngine` runs ~15 rules over system settings
  (`Settings.System`/`Settings.Global`), `PowerManager`, sync state, the drain
  stats, usage stats, and the OEM profile, and ranks the output by impact.

## Building

Open the project in Android Studio (Ladybug or newer) and run, or:

```
./gradlew assembleDebug
```

The APK lands in `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/`. Requires JDK 17+.
Min SDK 26 (Android 8.0), target SDK 35.

## Installing on your phone

1. Build the debug APK (or download it from CI if configured).
2. Copy it to the phone and open it; allow "install unknown apps" when asked.
3. Optional but recommended: grant **Usage access** from the Apps tab to
   unlock per-app consumption ranking.
4. Let it run for a day or two — drain rates and history-based suggestions
   sharpen as samples accumulate.

## Privacy

No `INTERNET` permission is declared, so the app *cannot* transmit anything.
History is stored in app-private storage and pruned after 30 days.
